# BLACKOUT | Immersive narrative mobile experience

**Independent product development · Design and prototype stage**

BLACKOUT explores atmospheric audio-led storytelling, investigations, vintage editorial interfaces and immersive narrative experiences. The user should feel present inside the story rather than navigating a generic content feed.

## Design and development approach

- Mobile-first, restrained visual interface and strong narrative atmosphere.
- Vintage newspaper / book-inspired typography, layers of evidence, letters and archives.
- Product and story integration: audio, interaction, discovery and pacing.
- Asset sourcing and rights review, alongside prototype iteration.

The design has evolved through several prototype iterations. The current interface and licensed assets remain private.

Private designs, code, story details and audio remain protected. The app has not been publicly released.
