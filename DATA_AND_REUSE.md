# Data, provenance and reuse

This portfolio differentiates archived scientific code and output, AI-assisted commercial research prototypes, a reconstructed stellar-analysis workflow and development-stage app case studies. The July gravity manuscript is unpublished; historical results are not newly rerun or peer reviewed by this repository update.

Financial data in Space Economics are factual extracts with original filing citations. The model and its scenarios are educational, not investment or lending advice.

Orbit Intelligence's historical AIS inputs were sourced from third-party republications. Full raw values and data-bearing dashboards are withheld pending redistribution permission. Aggregate error metrics are shown; any rerun requires legitimately obtained inputs in the documented format.

Astronomical observations and images remain subject to their survey providers' terms. Original source data must be obtained where permitted.

ATHAR and BLACKOUT private source code, licensed assets and story materials are not released here. Standard Chartered/Forage task inputs, templates and solutions are excluded. Completion information is not a new certificate.

No blanket licence to third-party material is granted.
