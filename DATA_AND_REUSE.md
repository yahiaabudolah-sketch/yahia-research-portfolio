# Data and reuse

Financial inputs in Space Economics are small factual extracts and independently structured calculations linked to annual filings. Original corporate filings and third-party datasets retain their respective rights; source URLs are included. The material is for educational research, not financial advice. No blanket licence to other parties' material is granted.

Orbit Intelligence's raw historical AIS series was obtained via third-party republication. The provider's permission to re-publish the full series was not confirmed. Accordingly, the raw data, hardcoded transcription, generated dashboard and detailed data-bearing outputs are excluded from the public package. The methodology, aggregate findings and local analysis code are provided. Refer to the data providers' terms and obtain permitted copies locally. Do not treat the optional optical extension as a completed experiment.

Forage task templates, model answers, client inputs, certificates, unpublished manuscripts and Blackout source files/assets are excluded.
