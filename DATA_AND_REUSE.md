# Data and provenance

Each project page describes its data source, methods and limitations. The commercial models were developed with AI assistance and await independent reproduction; the archived astrophysics results are not presented as new runs.

## Financial data

Space Economics uses figures extracted from annual filings. The scenarios are illustrative, not investment or lending advice.

## Maritime data

Orbit Intelligence uses historical AIS observations obtained from third-party republications. The raw series and dashboard are withheld pending redistribution permission. Aggregate results and the analysis code are available.

## Astronomy

Survey observations are subject to the providers' terms. The gravity study is unpublished. LAMOST contains a reconstructed workflow, not the original dataset.

## Product projects and certification

ATHAR and BLACKOUT source code and licensed assets are private. Forage task materials and certificate verification codes are not published.

Third-party materials retain their respective rights.
