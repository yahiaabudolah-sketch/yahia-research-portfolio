# ZTF & ATLAS | Time-domain astronomical analysis

An exploratory search for periodic signals and a subsequent effort to reject false positives using quality cuts, observing-season stability, same-exposure correlations and independent ATLAS photometry.

## Key observations

- A pair of neighbouring ZTF sources showed a same-night correlation of approximately 0.793 over 100 matched exposures, but the apparent periodic power was much weaker after exclusion of that night.
- The correlation was not unique in the field, so shared or local photometric systematics remain a plausible explanation.
- A separate candidate with a trial period of approximately 1.49927 days was evaluated in independent ATLAS photometry (4,262 input rows). The independent report does not establish persistent periodicity.
